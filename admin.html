Eggs recorded at relocation

Record three distinct egg categories from the TRACC datasheet. Only Eggs relocated will be shown to visitors after approval. Broken and deformed egg counts are staff-only. Blank means not yet recorded; zero means confirmed none.

Hatchling release outcome

Enter cumulative healthy and deformed hatchlings released before excavation (0 for confirmed none). Do not include the hatchlings recovered during excavation here. The total adds those recovered and released hatchlings separately.

Excavation outcome · staff only

Enter the seven counts when excavation is completed. Use 0 for confirmed none. Hatchlings are live recovered hatchlings actually released, not already included in healthy/deformed release counts. Dead Turtle means dead hatchlings found in the nest; embryos still in eggs belong in the appropriate egg stage. Only the combined release total is published.

Optional mother turtle information · public after approval

Mother details appear publicly only when at least one tag number is entered. Please confirm the tag and any published measurements are approved for sharing.

If not selected, tags and measurements remain staff-only. Publishing requires at least one tag number.

Custom nest metadata

Add, edit, or remove optional metadata for this nest. Each field has a label, value and visibility. Public fields appear only after administrator approval; protected fields remain staff-only. Never put GPS coordinates or sensitive information in a public field.

Protected research and nest-sheet fields (staff only)

These fields follow the attached datasheet and are saved separately from the public website. Reviewers can inspect them, but anonymous visitors cannot retrieve them.
